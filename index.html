About EcoCampus

Driving the sustainable future of Cagayan de Oro City through smart, student-led waste management.

Our Mission

The EcoCampus Waste Management Initiative was established to create a 100% sustainable and zero-waste university environment. By utilizing digital mapping technology, we empower students, faculty, and the administration to dispose of waste responsibly and track our environmental impact in real-time.

Our Vision

We envision our university as the leading green institution in Northern Mindanao, serving as a model for urban sustainability where technology meets environmental consciousness.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common waste management queries on campus.

What exactly goes into the Recyclable (Green) bin? +

Only clean, dry, and recyclable materials. This includes plastic bottles (PET), aluminum cans, dry paper, and cardboard. Note: Please empty all liquids from bottles before throwing them away to prevent contamination!

I found a garbage bin that isn't on the map. What should I do? +

You can add it yourself! Go to the Map Dashboard, click on the exact location on the campus map to register the area, and use the "Add New Bin" form on the left panel to specify the bin type and its specific location.

What goes into the Residual (Red) bin? +

Residual waste is anything that cannot be recycled or composted. This includes candy wrappers, soiled food packaging, sanitary napkins, wet wipes, and single-use sachets.

Who manages the collection of these bins? +

The University Auxiliary Services handles the physical collection. Data from this map helps them prioritize areas with high waste generation. Bins in high-traffic zones are emptied twice a day.
